About This Project

The AI-Based Budget Utilization Monitoring System is a centralized digital platform that tracks budget allocation, expenditure, and utilization rates in real time. It uses rule-based detection logic (with optional AI enhancements) to identify anomalies such as sudden spending spikes, prolonged inactivity of allocated funds, and deviation from approved budgets.

Problem Solved:

  • Lack of real-time visibility into budget utilization
  • Delayed identification of under-utilized funds
  • Risk of fund leakage or irregular spending patterns
  • Manual reporting errors
  • Limited transparency in financial workflows
  • Difficulty in ensuring accountability across departments
